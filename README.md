# YSM Archives

**A version-pinned archive of Yokaiste's Sandbox Mod (YSM) and its collaborations for [WARNO](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1611600/WARNO/).**
Every build here is tied to a specific WARNO game version, so you can always install the mod build that matches the exact version of WARNO you're running.

YSM Community Discord: https://discord.gg/8AMXxnjngR

## Get the mods

You don't need to download from here by hand — use the tools that read this archive automatically:

**[Yuri's WARNO Toolkit](https://github.com/dary1337/yuri-warno-toolkit)** — a Windows app that installs these builds for you and edits your WARNO battlegroups directly on your profile.

- **One-click install** — finds your WARNO (Steam or non-Steam), picks the build here that matches your exact game version, backs up your current setup first, and rolls back if anything fails. Covers YSM (including the 2.0), WTO, YSM × WiF, and YSM × WiF × WTO — and can re-activate mods you've already downloaded, including ones from the Steam Workshop.
- **Version switching** — downgrade or change your WARNO build in one click so it matches the mod you want to run.
- **Battlegroup editor** — reads every deck on your profile, *including the ones WARNO hides* (built under another mod or an older game build), and lets you edit units, veterancy and transports; rename, duplicate or delete decks; migrate decks between mods and builds; or build new ones from scratch — even decks too heavy to share by in-game code.
- **Profile backups** — snapshot and restore your whole profile; every change is written straight to your profile, backed up and verified first.
